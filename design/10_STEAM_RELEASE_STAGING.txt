1. THE "CODED-FIRST" RELEASE & ANNOUNCEMENT PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To protect developer sanity and maintain 100% credibility with the community:

RULE 1: ZERO PROMISED TIMELINES OR SCHEDULES
- NEVER tell the public or subscribers "weekly updates", "drops every Friday", 
  or promise exact calendar dates.
- Updates drop on Steam purely at developer discretion when the current version 
  has had enough breathing room and feedback.

RULE 2: THE "CODED-FIRST" PUBLIC TEASER MANDATE
- NEVER list a feature in public "Upcoming Features" (Steam descriptions, Discord 
  announcements, or community teasers) unless it is ALREADY 100% coded, compiled, 
  and tested in our Git staging repository.
- Distant design concepts, ideas, and unbuilt systems remain strictly in private 
  internal documentation (`design/08_DIPLOMACY_INTEGRATION.txt`).
- Public announcements only announce what is already sitting "in the chamber", 
  guaranteeing zero missed promises or delayed expectations.

RULE 3: INCREMENTAL SINGLE-FEATURE RELEASES
- Releasing one completed feature per version makes every update feel substantial, 
  bumps the mod into Steam's "Recently Updated" feed, and isolates bug tracking 
  to a single isolated domain service.

STEAM UPDATE #1: v1.4.0 — "The Ground Zero Survival Update"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steam Change Note text:
Realms of Dust — Update v1.4.0: "The Ground Zero Survival Update"
================================================================

Thanks for checking out Realms of Dust! While waiting for Steam Workshop approval, 
I've been heavily playtesting and tuning early-game survival and economics. 
Here is what's new in v1.4.0:

Starter Retinues (Anti-Ambush Buffer):
- No more getting jumped by 12 looters while alone on horseback on Day 1.
- You now start with an immediate cultural retinue scaled to your difficulty:
  * Realistic / Bannerlord: 10x Tier-2 Shielded Infantry (holds a tight shield wall).
  * Warrior: 6x T1, 3x T2, 1x T3.
  * Freebooter: 4x T1, 4x T2, 4x T3.

Bandit Horde Mobility & Logistics:
- Merged bandit hordes (20+ troops) now receive pack mounts (1 per 5 bandits) so they 
  don't crawl at a snail pace when carrying captured loot and prisoners.
- Set a minimum map speed floor of 3.6 so large hordes move deliberately, while 
  healthy caravans (4.8+) and player scouts can still easily outrun them.

2-Phase Seasonal Bandit Caps:
- Bandits scale progressively across the seasons: Summer (15 -> 30), Autumn (30 -> 40), 
  Winter (45 -> 55), and Year 2+ (65 -> 75).

Capital Loyalty & Culture Fix:
- Fixed early capital rebellions by matching AI clans to their capital's culture 
  and adding a Year 1 civic stabilization buffer while garrisons rebuild.

STEAM UPDATE #2: v1.5.0 — "Rogue Deserters & Outlaw Companies"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steam Change Note text:
Realms of Dust — Update v1.5.0: "Rogue Deserters & Outlaw Companies"
===================================================================

This update introduces disciplined military deserter warbands, bridging the gap 
between early peasant looters and late-game kingdom warfare.

Season 0 Grace Period:
- Deserters are completely suppressed during Season 0 (Summer / Days 1-21) to give 
  players and AI lords time to rebuild, clear bandits, and train starter retinues.
- Deserters begin appearing at the start of Season 1 (Autumn / Day 22+).

Anti-Doomstack Merge Rules:
- Only small splinter bands (<= 25 troops) can merge together for mutual survival.
- Once a deserter company reaches 40+ troops, they lock and stop absorbing smaller bands, 
  preventing one giant blob from vacuuming up every rogue in the territory.
- Hard ceiling capped at 60 troops max (overriding vanilla's 120-man blob).

Tactical Combat & Military Loot:
- Deserters fight in disciplined shield walls, spear braces, and ranged volleys.
- Yields authentic military equipment (chainmail, kite shields, military blades, horses) 
  and allows recruiting captured veterans directly into your army.
- Outlaw neutral alignment: zero diplomatic fallout or kingdom wars from attacking them.

STEAM UPDATE #3: v1.6.0 — "Castle Strongholds & Realm Patrols"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steam Change Note text:
Realms of Dust — Update v1.6.0: "Castle Strongholds & Realm Patrols"
===================================================================

Say goodbye to external patrol mod conflicts! Realms of Dust now features native, 
integrated settlement patrols designed specifically for our 53 micro-realms.

Castle Guard House Parity:
- Castles can now construct the Guard House settlement project, giving military 
  strongholds a dedicated defensive purpose.

Automated Village Patrols:
- Completing Tier 1 of the Guard House immediately deploys a dedicated garrison patrol 
  outside the castle gates.
- Patrols stay tethered to their castle and bound villages, defending farmers and 
  caravans from roaming bandits while leaving the wildlands open for you to hunt.

Seasonal Headcount Scaling:
- Level 1 Guard House: 15-20 troops (Tier 2-3 shield footmen & archers).
- Level 2 Guard House: 30-35 troops (Tier 3-4 armored spearmen & crossbows).
- Level 3 Guard House: 50-60 troops (Tier 4-5 veterans + scout cavalry).
- 24-Hour Muster: If wiped out, castles automatically muster a replacement after 24 hours.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEAM UPDATE #4: v1.7.0 — "Frontline Border Wars"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steam Change Note text:
Realms of Dust — Update v1.7.0: "Frontline Border Wars"
======================================================

In standard Bannerlord, AI micro-kingdoms declare war randomly across the entire map. 
v1.7.0 brings sanity and structure to Calradia's borders.

Border Contiguity War Filter:
- AI kingdoms can now ONLY declare war on immediate neighbors sharing physical borders 
  (settlements within ~60 map distance units).
- Eliminates incoherent cross-continent wars (no more Aserai declaring war on Sturgia 
  across 15 neutral realms).
- Creates distinct regional frontlines, border clashes, and localized spheres of power.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEAM UPDATE #5: v1.8.0 — "The 21-Day Post-War Truce Accord"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steam Change Note text:
Realms of Dust — Update v1.8.0: "The 21-Day Post-War Truce Accord"
=================================================================

No more AI factions signing peace, taking your tribute, and declaring war again 
48 hours later.

Mandatory 21-Day Non-Aggression Pacts:
- Signing a peace treaty now enforces a hard 21-in-game-day diplomatic cooldown.
- Neither party can re-declare war during this grace window.
- Gives realms guaranteed time to recruit, rebuild shattered garrisons, and let trade recover.

When you are ready to publish an update:

Step 2: Steam Workshop Publish (ONLY AFTER MODERATION CLEARS!)

  - Update "changenote" with the corresponding pre-written text from Section 3 above.
  - Run: "steamcmd\upload.bat"
