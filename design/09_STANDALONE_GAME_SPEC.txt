================================================================================
STANDALONE GAME SPECIFICATION: REALMS OF DUST (TACTICAL TURN-BASED RPG)
Universe Overview, Core Mechanics, and Architecture Bible
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & FRANCHISE CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Realms of Dust" is an independent, turn-based tactical mercenary RPG set in 
the same gritty, low-fantasy universe as the Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord 
overhaul mod of the same name.

While the Bannerlord mod serves as the high-level "Macro" conquest funnel (20+ 
micro-realms waging continent-scale war), the Standalone Game is the intimate 
"Micro" perspective: following a small 4-character mercenary company taking contracts, 
surviving dangerous roads, managing rations, and fighting in deep tactical WeGo 
squad battles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SHARED DNA & ASSET ECOSYSTEM (MOD VS. STANDALONE PARITY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The standalone game directly mirrors the core itemization, culture, and faction 
balance of the universe:
- 6 Cultural Factions: Exact lore, cultural identity, and aesthetic archetypes 
  (desert nomads, highlanders, imperial legions, etc.) with custom tailored names.
- Equipment & Item Catalog: 1:1 mirroring of weapons, armor classes, mounts, 
  and trade goods (grain, iron, flax, salt, pack sumpters).
- Unit Archetypes as Discrete Enemies: Rather than upgrading dynamic player troop 
  trees, the game uses the faction troop trees as discrete, fixed enemy profiles 
  (e.g., Tier 1 Levy, Tier 3 Armored Footman, Tier 5 Cataphract/Knight) that do 
  not dynamically transform mid-campaign.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. OVERWORLD TRAVEL & ENCOUNTER SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 2D Top-Down World Map: Clean, flat, top-down perspective without the overhead 
  or camera clutter of 3D terrain.
- Dynamic Threat Level Meter: As the party travels across regional wilderness 
  and roads, a Threat Meter accumulates based on territory danger.
- Skill-Based Evasion / Slip-Away: When an encounter rolls (bandits, deserters, 
  patrols), the player is not forced to fight blindly. High party scouting/evasion 
  skills allow slipping away into the terrain, mirroring Bannerlord's campaign 
  party speed escape mechanic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SQUAD COMBAT SYSTEM: 4-LANE WEGO (SIMULTANEOUS RESOLUTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike standard JRPG "You Go, I Go" turns or Bannerlord's hands-off "Send Troops", 
combat is an active, turn-based tactical 4-lane skirmish with WeGo execution:

A. 4-Lane Battle Grid (Front & Back Ranks):
   - 4 discrete combat lanes for both friendly squad (max 4 characters) and enemies.
   - Frontline: Direct melee engagement (can only strike the lane directly opposite).
   - Backline: Ranged and support units.

B. Ranged Geometry & Line-of-Sight:
   - Crossbows: High armor penetration, can shoot straight or across 1 adjacent lane.
   - Bows: Arcing trajectory, can shoot across up to 2 lanes for flexible artillery.

C. Flanking Risk & Line Collapse Dynamics:
   - Flank Penalty: Focusing multiple frontliners into a single enemy lane leaves 
     uncovered lanes open to severe flanking penalties from unblocked enemies.
   - Line Collapse: Wiping out an entire front line forces backline archers and 
     support units to move into melee range in the front line.

D. WeGo Resolution (Simultaneous Turns):
   - Both player and enemy queue actions simultaneously and secretly.
   - Actions resolve together based on initiative, creating psychological reads 
     (e.g., predicting an enemy shield block vs. incoming thrust).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. INTERACTIVE 1V1 DUELS & JOUSTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Breaks up tactical squad battles with intense, reflex-driven real-time mini-games:
- Static Visual Slicing: Uses static character/horse art with dynamic horizontal 
  and vertical slivers/slits cut out (simulating restricted helmet visor slits 
  and lance alignment rails).
- Precision Timing & Reflex Meters: Real-time mouse click timing controls 
  strike accuracy, shield parries, and lance unhorsing.
- Contexts: Used for formal tournament jousts, trials by combat, and noble 1v1 
  duels.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. STRATEGIC PURPOSE & SOLO DEV PRIORITIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The Bannerlord mod is an audience acquisition and community funnel.
- The Standalone Game is the commercial indie title.
- Bannerlord mod updates must remain lightweight and disciplined (<200 LOC per file) 
  so the developer can protect core creative focus for the standalone game's 
  turn-based engine, lane combat algorithms, and companion progression.
