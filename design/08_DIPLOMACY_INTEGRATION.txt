================================================================================
REALMS OF DUST: DIPLOMACY INTEGRATION SPECIFICATION
Eliminating Third-Party Diplomacy Dependencies in Fractured Calradia
================================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE & PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In standard Bannerlord, players rely heavily on external "Diplomacy" mods to fix
erratic AI wars, instant re-declarations, and absent peace logic. In Realms of
Dust, where Calradia is fractured into 20+ equal micro-realms, vanilla diplomacy
fails even harder. 

Our goal is to build the high-value diplomatic rules directly into Realms of Dust
using pure C# domain services (under 200 lines per file, zero custom Gauntlet UI
bloat), rendering external diplomacy mods completely unnecessary.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TARGET FEATURES TO ABSORB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. BORDER CONTIGUITY / GEOGRAPHICAL CONTACT WAR DECLARATION
   - Problem: Vanilla AI micro-kingdoms declare war across the continent (e.g., 
     an Aserai micro-realm declaring war on a Sturgian micro-realm with 15 kingdoms
     in between).
   - Solution: AI factions can ONLY declare war if they share direct physical 
     borders (settlement proximity under a tunable distance threshold, e.g., 
     60 map units).
   - Value: Creates localized border skirmishes, frontlines, and regional wars
     instead of global incoherent chaos.

B. MANDATORY TRUCE COOLDOWNS (NON-AGGRESSION PERIODS)
   - Problem: Vanilla AI frequently declares war 1 or 2 days after paying tribute
     for peace.
   - Solution: Hard diplomatic cooldown (tunable, default: 21 in-game days) 
     following any peace agreement where neither party can declare war on each 
     other without an extreme influence/honor penalty or hard block.
   - Value: Gives players and AI realms time to rebuild garrisons, recruit, and 
     manage economic recovery after a war.

C. WAR EXHAUSTION & ATTRITION SYSTEM
   - Problem: Wars drag on perpetually until one side is completely annexed.
   - Solution: Track a normalized War Exhaustion score (0 to 100%) calculated from:
     * Casualties suffered in battles.
     * Settlements and bound villages raided or captured.
     * Clan leaders captured/held in dungeons.
     * Duration of the war.
   - When Exhaustion hits 100%, the faction demands/accepts white peace or tribute 
     scaled to relative performance.

D. MESSENGER DISPATCH SYSTEM (LIGHTWEIGHT DIALOGUE ACCESS)
   - Problem: Players must hunt across the continent for weeks just to talk to a 
     king or lord to propose marriage, peace, or trade.
   - Solution: A simple domain service allowing the player to send an emissary 
     from their settlement to any known lord. After an arrival delay (e.g., 1-2 days), 
     a dialogue encounter screen opens directly.
   - Value: Solves 80% of why players install Diplomacy mods without needing 
     complex custom UI windows.

E. EXPANSIONISM & COALITION BALANCING (ANTI-SNOWBALL)
   - Problem: If one micro-realm captures 3-4 towns early, they snowball the map.
   - Solution: Each conquered settlement adds an "Expansion Aggression" penalty. 
     Neighboring micro-realms form temporary defensive pacts against runaway 
     conquerors, maintaining continent-wide equilibrium.

F. [COMPLETED - v1.1.0] DIFFICULTY-SCALED STARTING RETINUE (VETERAN RETINUE QUALITY SCALING)
   - Problem: Basic Tier 1 levies have virtually zero armor and terrible combat 
     stats; on Bannerlord difficulty, 10 raw recruits get slaughtered by 11 looters 
     hurling rocks.
   - Solution: Keep a lean, tight party footprint (10-12 troops) across difficulties, 
     but scale the QUALITY / TIER of the retinue based on game difficulty:
     * Freebooter (Easy): 12 troops with seasoned veteran backbone:
       - 4x Tier 1 Levies / Recruits
       - 4x Tier 2 Footmen / Skirmishers
       - 4x Tier 3 Veteran Infantry / Archers (armored frontline protection)
     * Warrior (Medium): 10 troops with light veteran support:
       - 6x Tier 1 Levies / Recruits
       - 3x Tier 2 Footmen
       - 1x Tier 3 Veteran
     * Bannerlord (Hardest): 10 troops raw levy survival:
       - 8x Tier 1 Levies / Recruits
       - 2x Tier 2 Basic Footmen
   - Value: Ensures a player who starts on easier/medium modes has competent 
     bodyguards capable of surviving early skirmishes and protecting the lord.

G. [COMPLETED - v1.4.0] BANDIT HORDE MOBILITY & PACK MOUNT PROVISIONING
   - Problem: Large merged bandit hordes (30-60 troops) crawl at 2.5-3.0 speed 
     due to pure infantry weight and looted cargo, making them look sluggish and 
     harmless to caravans.
   - Solution: 
     * Provision merged hordes with a small ration of cheap sumpter/pack horses 
       proportional to their merged size (e.g., 1 pack horse per 4-5 bandits).
     * Establish a reasonable minimum speed floor (3.6) so hordes move 
       deliberately without outrunning light scouts.

H. SETTLEMENT REALM GUARDS & PATROL PARTIES (ABSORBING "BETTER PATROLS")
   - Problem: Pre-activated hideouts, 55+ merged hordes, and enemy raiding lords 
     massacre bound villages and trade routes without local territorial defense.
   - Solution (Realms of Dust Native Architecture):
     1. Castle Guard House Parity: Unlock the Guard House settlement project for 
        both Towns AND Castles (ensuring each realm's stronghold protects its villages).
     2. Horde-Synchronized Scaling: Patrol party size scales with seasonal horde caps:
        * Guard House L1: ~50% seasonal cap (15-25 troops).
        * Guard House L2: ~75% seasonal cap (30-40 troops).
        * Guard House L3: Matches or exceeds seasonal cap (50-60 troops).
     3. Armored Quality Composition: Seed patrols with Tier 3-5 shielded cultural 
        soldiers capable of cutting through hordes of unarmored looters in battle.
     4. Rapid Muster & Smart Territorial Defense:
        * 24-hour respawn window following a wipe.
        * Intercepts enemy raiding lords, reinforces battles near settlement gates, 
          and captures shattered enemy parties to throw in dungeons.
   - Value: Eliminates dependency on external patrol mods while perfectly balancing 
     against our aggressive bandit horde ecosystem.

I. ROGUE DESERTER COMPANIES (SOVEREIGN PVE COMBAT & MID-TIER GEAR BRIDGE)
   - Problem: Starting on Day 1 as a sovereign kingdom removes the vanilla 
     mercenary freelance grind loop. Players cannot safely fight kingdom armies 
     without triggering existential wars that risk their capital. Meanwhile, merged 
     looter hordes provide troop headcounts but throw rocks, fight without formations, 
     and drop crude vendor junk (sickles and rags) instead of martial combat gear.
     Furthermore, in vanilla Bannerlord, deserters rarely spawn, and when they do, 
     they merge uncontrollably into 120-man blobs, vacuuming up every small band 
     and leaving the rest of the map empty.
   - Solution & Custom Mechanics:
     1. Season 0 Suppression (The Rebuilding Grace):
        * Deserters are completely suppressed during Season 0 (Summer / Days 1-21).
        * Allows realms and players to rebuild from the Ground Zero reset, clear 
          starter bandit hideouts, and train initial retinues without early military wipes.
        * Deserters begin appearing at the end of the first season / start of Season 1 (Day 22+).
     2. The 25-Troop Merge Eligibility Rule:
        * Only small splinter bands (<= 25 troops) are eligible to merge into another party.
        * Ensures small scattered remnants band together for survival (e.g., 20 + 25 -> 45).
     3. The 40-Troop Company Lock Rule:
        * Once a deserter company reaches 40+ troops, they are considered an "established 
          battalion" and become LOCKED from absorbing smaller parties.
        * Prevents the vanilla "Black Hole" effect where one giant stack swallows every 
          deserter in the territory, ensuring the map retains a healthy variety of small 
          (15-25) and large (40-60) targets.
     4. Strict 60-Man Hard Cap:
        * Overrides vanilla's 120-man merge limit with a strict hard ceiling of 60 troops.
        * Provides a formidable, epic mid-game field skirmish for 35-50 man player armies 
          without ever snowballing into an unbeatable 100+ man doomstack.
     5. Outlaw Neutral Alignment & Tactical Combat:
        * Deserters are unaligned brigands; attacking them causes zero diplomatic 
          blowback, zero war declarations, and zero clan feuds.
        * Disciplined formations (shield walls, spear braces against cavalry, ranged volleys).
     6. High-Value Martial Loot & Veteran Replacements:
        * Yields real military armor (chainmail, kite shields, military blades, riding mounts).
        * Captured deserters can be recruited directly as seasoned Tier 3-4 frontline veterans.
   - Value: Bridges the gap between early peasant looters and late-game kingdom warfare, 
     providing sovereign lords a dangerous, rewarding, and consequence-free combat grind.

4. ARCHITECTURAL & COMPONENT BREAKDOWN (REALMS OF DUST MANDATES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All components adhere to the <200 LOC per file limit and native save persistence:

1. RealmsOfDust.Core:
   - IDiplomacyService.cs: Interface for war declarations, truces, and exhaustion.
   - DiplomacyConfig.cs: Tunable constants (TruceDays, ExhaustionWeights, Distance).
   - Models/WarRecord.cs: Relational tracking of casualties, raids, and dates.
   - RealmsOfDustConstants.cs: Mobility & mount tuning constants. [COMPLETED - v1.4.0]
   - RealmsOfDustConstants.cs: Deserter merge & lock constants. [COMPLETED - v1.5.0]

2. RealmsOfDust.Domain:
   - PlayerStartingRetinueService.cs: Difficulty-aware culture recruit seeder. [COMPLETED - v1.1.0]
   - BanditHordeMountService.cs: Provisions pack animals & transfers loot on merge. [COMPLETED - v1.4.0]
   - PostPlaguePartySpeedModel.cs: Enforces 3.6 minimum map speed floor for merged hordes. [COMPLETED - v1.4.0]
   - DeserterCompanyService.cs: Spawns and manages roaming Tier 3-4 rogue military warbands. [COMPLETED - v1.5.0]
   - WarExhaustionService.cs: Daily calculation of war fatigue.
   - BorderProximityWarFilter.cs: Distance checks validating eligible war targets.
   - TruceCooldownService.cs: Enforcing peace treaty durations.
   - AntiSnowballCoalitionService.cs: Dynamic neighbor threat calculations.

3. RealmsOfDust.Engine:
   - RealmsOfDustDeserterBehavior.cs: Enforces Season 0 grace and 60-man deserter limits. [COMPLETED - v1.5.0]
   - RealmsOfDustDiplomacyBehavior.cs: Subscribes to OnWarDeclared, OnPeaceDeclared,
     OnSettlementOwnerChanged, and DailyTick.
   - SaveableTypeDefiner: Registers diplomatic models for native save compatibility.

2. Zero compatibility bugs with third-party diplomacy UI updates.
3. Completely seamless "one-click install" experience on Steam Workshop.
