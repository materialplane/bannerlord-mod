A. v1.1.0 & v1.1.1 — Starter Troops & Early Balancing:

   - Bandit hordes merge up to dynamic caps in `BanditHordeMergeService.cs`:

     - Autumn (S1): Days 22-31 (Cap 30), Days 32-42 (Cap 40).
     - Winter (S2): Days 43-52 (Cap 45), Days 53-63 (Cap 55).
     - Spring (S3 / Yr 2+): Days 64-73 (Cap 65), Days 74+ (Cap 75).
   - Second-half pressure ensures roaming bandits stay +5 above player caps.

C. v1.3.0 — Difficulty-Aware Personal Party Size & Sovereign Unlocks:
   - Realistic Mode: Hard-capped at 25 in Season 0. Personal bonuses from Steward skill (+0.25/level)
     and combat/leadership perks (Merry Men, Horde Leader, Authority, etc.) unlock in Season 1 (Autumn 35 bump),
     stacking directly on top of the 35 baseline.
   - Easy Mode: Personal Steward and perk bonuses are unlocked from Day 1.
   - Sovereign Kingdom Leader Bonus (+20): Suppressed during Year 1 (Days 1-84) for all realms to represent
     post-plague crown rebuilding. Unlocks in Year 2+.
   - Created `PostPlagueBonusCalculator.cs` and refactored `PostPlaguePartySizeLimitModel.cs`.
   - Fixed save loading freeze: Wrapped all perk and campaign option lookups in defensive try/catch guards
     so they never throw NullReferenceException during early save deserialization.

D. v1.3.1 — Settlement Rebellion & Culture-Clash Fix:
   - Issue: 4 capitals (out of 53, ~7.5%) were rebelling in Month 1 due to vanilla culture mismatch (-3 loyalty/day)
     and low 20-man garrisons.
   - Fix 1 (`FactionElevationService.cs`): AI clan assignment now strictly prioritizes noble clans matching
     the capital's exact culture.
   - Fix 2 (`PostPlagueSettlementLoyaltyModel.cs`): Added Year 1 civic loyalty model:
     - Softens cross-culture penalty by +2.0.
     - Adds a +1.0 civic stabilization buffer against early low-garrison security/food dips.
     - Registered in `RealmsOfDustSubModule.cs`.
